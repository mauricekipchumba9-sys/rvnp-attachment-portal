Insurance cover

Start typing your name or admission number (at least 3 letters). Pick yourself from the suggestions to get your cover.

Fill in your details exactly as they appear on your student records. The Industrial Liaison Office will prepare your cover. Come back to this page later and search for your name to download it.

Placement

Once you have secured a place for your attachment, fill in the details below. After the Industrial Liaison Office assigns you a trainer (supervisor), come back here, enter your name and admission number, and press Check my trainer.

Describe the location clearly so your trainer can find it.
Submitting again with the same name and admission number updates your details. If you change the organization or town, your trainer will be assigned again.

My marks

Your marks are private. You can only see your own, after signing in with your PIN. Nobody can search for or open another student's marks.

Create your PIN once. To prove it is you, enter the phone number you gave on the student details form (the same one the Industrial Liaison Office has). Keep your PIN to yourself.

Forgot your PIN? Ask the Industrial Liaison Office to reset it. Five wrong PINs lock sign-in for 15 minutes.
Your marks are hidden automatically when you leave this tab. Marks are entered by your ILO representative. If something looks wrong, tell them.
