Insurance cover for a class

Tell the Industrial Liaison Office which insurance policy a class is covered by. Once approved, it is applied to every student of that class at once, and the policy number on their attachment letter and insurance cover will match.

Enter student marks

Search a student by name or admission number, then enter the mark. Students see only their own marks, after signing in with their own PIN. Only students who filled the student details form can be found.

Saving the same unit and assessment again for this student replaces the earlier mark, so nothing is entered twice. The unit, assessment and "out of" stay filled in so you can enter the next student quickly.
